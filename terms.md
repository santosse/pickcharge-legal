## 1. What the App does

time. The App has no accounts — access is subscription-based, described
in the next section.

## 3. Estimates are estimates, not guarantees

**This is the most important section — please read it.** The App helps
you decide where to charge, which means its numbers can influence a
real driving decision. You should understand their limits:

- **Station data comes from a third-party source** — Portugal's national
  EV charging network (MOBI.E), via the National Access Point operated on
  behalf of IMT. We don't control that data, and it can be incomplete,
  delayed, or wrong (a charger showing "available" may not be by the time
  you arrive; a listed price may have changed).
- **Distance and reachability estimates use straight-line distance**, not
  actual road distance — real driving distance can be meaningfully
  longer, especially in cities or hilly terrain.
- **Cost and charging-time estimates use average power and consumption
  figures**, not your vehicle's real-time charging curve — actual cost
  and time can differ from the estimate.
- **Always leave a safety margin.** Don't rely on the App's arrival-state-
  of-charge estimate as a guarantee you'll reach a charger — factor in
  your own judgment, driving style, weather, and terrain.

We provide this information "as is," without warranty of accuracy, and
are not liable for consequences of relying on it — including, without
limitation, being unable to reach a charging station.
